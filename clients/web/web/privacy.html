Personal Data Governance & Privacy Architecture
This Privacy Policy details the technical and organizational mechanisms through which ConnectHub processes, stores, and protects organizational communications, device telemetry, and personal data.

1. Scope & Privacy Principles

ConnectHub processes personal and institutional data in conformity with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and enterprise security best practices. We maintain strict organizational boundaries with an unconditional zero commercial sale policy.

2. Information Collected & Processed

Data processed includes: (a) Account info (name, handle, email, Argon2 password hashes, avatar); (b) Device telemetry (IP address, browser ID, OS, mobile device identifiers); (c) Session tokens (persistent JWT Bearer tokens for "Keep me signed in"); (d) Chat communications (messages, attachments, pins, reactions); and (e) Immutable compliance audit logs.

3. Device Storage & "Keep Me Signed In"

Enabling "Keep me signed in" saves persistent JWT credentials in your device storage to maintain active sessions across browser restarts and device reboots. To terminate persistent device storage, users can perform an explicit manual logout.

4. Zero-Leak Group Partitioning

ConnectHub enforces strict mathematical and programmatic isolation. Non-members cannot discover, search, or access private group metadata or messages. Administrative access is logged in the permanent audit trail.

5. Zero Commercial Sale Guarantee

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or disclose user personal data, telemetry, or message transcripts to third-party advertisers or data brokers. All data remains strictly within your enterprise tenant.

6. Security Architecture & Encryption

The platform employs Transport Layer Security (TLS), Argon2 password hashing, and server-side RBAC validation to prevent unauthorized access and protect institutional data.

7. Data Principal Rights & Administrative Requests

Users can request access, correction, or deactivation of account records by contacting their designated university / enterprise Organization IT Administrator or Platform Super Administrator.
